About

OpenRailTracker

Live rail tracking for passenger, commuter, freight, railcam, and service-condition context across North America.

Mission

Make rail status readable.

The app combines live vehicle feeds, route geometry, station context, train history, speed points, and alerts into one map-first workspace.

Data Quality

Operator feeds vary.

Some agencies publish rich real-time arrivals and alerts; others only publish location snapshots. ORT shows what is available and avoids inventing schedules.

Accounts

Google sign-in only.

Account identity will be handled through Google authentication. ORT will not ship a separate password system.

FAQ

Why does a train sometimes jump?

Rail operators send updates at different intervals. Precision movement smooths known positions while the live marker still respects the latest source update.

Why do some railcams open differently?

Public camera hosts use different players. ORT opens playable sources in-app and keeps the source page available when a host blocks direct embedding.

Are alerts attached to every train?

No. Alerts should be train-specific when the source names a route or train number, and broader service notices stay in the dedicated alerts surface.

Can I use ORT as an official travel source?

No. Confirm boarding, fares, and operational decisions with the rail operator. ORT is a tracking and awareness tool.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

OpenRailTracker is designed around public rail data, local preferences, and explicit user controls.

Overview

OpenRailTracker provides rail tracking, alerts, and railcam context using public transportation data. This policy explains how data is handled in the desktop and web experiences.

Information We Collect

  • Device location (optional): Only if you enable the location toggle to show your position on the map.
  • Local preferences: Theme, layers, refresh interval, and history toggles saved in local storage on your device.
  • Usage signals: Basic diagnostic logs to keep the app stable (no personal identifiers attached).

How We Use Data

  • Render live trains, routes, and station information on the map.
  • Remember your settings for a consistent experience.
  • Improve stability and performance.

Sharing & Third Parties

OpenRailTracker does not sell personal data. Railcam playback may load third-party video providers; their privacy policies apply to their players.

Data Retention

Local preferences remain on your device until you clear browser or app storage. Location is not stored unless you explicitly save it as part of a sighting submission.

Your Choices

  • Disable location at any time from system settings or the in-app toggle.
  • Clear stored preferences by clearing local storage or reinstalling the desktop app.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the OpenRailTracker support inbox.
